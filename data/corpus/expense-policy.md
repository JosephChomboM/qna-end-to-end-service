# Meridian Pay — Expense Policy

## Submitting Expenses

All business expenses must be submitted through the company expense tool within 30 days
of the purchase. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be denied.

## Approval Thresholds

Any single expense under 100 dollars is auto-approved. Expenses between 100 and 1,000
dollars require manager approval. Any expense over 1,000 dollars requires both manager
and finance team approval.

## Travel

Employees traveling for work may book economy-class flights for any trip. Business-class
flights are permitted only for flights longer than six hours, with director approval.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while traveling is 75 dollars per day. Alcohol is not
reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Reimbursement Timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days and paid by direct deposit.

## Corporate Card

Employees in sales and leadership roles are issued a corporate card. All other employees
pay out of pocket and submit for reimbursement.
