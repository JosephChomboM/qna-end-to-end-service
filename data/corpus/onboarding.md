# Meridian Pay — New Hire Onboarding

## Before Day One

New hires receive their company laptop by mail at least three business days before their
start date. IT ships the laptop already configured with the required software.

## First Week

Every new hire completes a structured first-week onboarding plan. This includes security
training, a product overview, and one-on-one introductions with their immediate team.

## Onboarding Buddy

Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team. The buddy checks in
with the new hire at least once a week for the first month.

## Required Training

All new hires must complete security awareness training within their first five business
days. Employees who handle customer data must also complete data-handling training in the
same period.

## 30-60-90 Plan

Managers create a 30-60-90 day plan for every new hire before the start date. The plan
sets clear goals for the first 30, 60, and 90 days of employment.

## Benefits Enrollment

New hires must enroll in health benefits within 30 days of their start date. Coverage
begins on the first day of the month after enrollment is completed.
