# Product Spec — Meridian Business Card

## Overview

The Meridian Business Card is a physical and virtual debit card tied directly to a
Meridian Wallet. Spending on the card draws down the Wallet balance in real time.

## Cash Back

The Business Card earns 1.5 percent cash back on all purchases. Cash back is deposited
into the customer's Wallet at the end of each month.

## Virtual Cards

Each customer can create up to 50 virtual cards at no cost. Virtual cards are used for
online purchases and can be frozen or deleted instantly.

## Spending Controls

Admins can set a per-card spending limit and restrict a card to specific merchant
categories. Limits can be set per transaction, per day, or per month.

## ATM Withdrawals

The Business Card can be used at any ATM. Meridian Pay charges no ATM fee, but the ATM
operator's fee still applies. The daily ATM withdrawal limit is 1,000 dollars.

## Replacement

A lost or stolen card can be frozen instantly from the app. A replacement physical card
is mailed within five to seven business days at no charge.
