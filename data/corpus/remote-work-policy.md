# Meridian Pay — Remote Work Policy

Meridian Pay is a remote-first company. All roles are eligible to work remotely from
anywhere within the United States.

## Home Office Stipend

Every new employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 1,000 dollars to purchase
equipment such as a desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days
of the start date.

## Internet Reimbursement

Meridian Pay reimburses employees 50 dollars per month toward home internet costs. This
reimbursement is added to the second paycheck of each month.

## Co-working Spaces

Employees who prefer not to work from home may expense a co-working membership up to 250
dollars per month with manager approval.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop to every employee. Employees may choose between a MacBook
Pro and a Dell XPS laptop. Laptops are refreshed every three years.

## Time Zones

While employees may live anywhere in the United States, all employees must overlap with
the 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM Central Time core collaboration window.
