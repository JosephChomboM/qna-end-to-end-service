# Meridian Pay — Company Overview

Meridian Pay is a financial technology company headquartered in Austin, Texas. The
company was founded in 2019 and builds digital payment products for small businesses.

Meridian Pay currently employs 180 people. The company operates as a fully remote-first
organization, with the Austin office used only for optional in-person gatherings.

The company's mission is to make business payments as simple as sending a text message.
Meridian Pay is privately held and has raised a total of 45 million dollars in venture
funding across two rounds.

The three core products are the Meridian Wallet, Meridian Transfers, and the Meridian
Business Card. All three share a single customer account.

The current CEO is Dana Okafor, who has led the company since it was founded. The
company's fiscal year runs from January 1 to December 31.
