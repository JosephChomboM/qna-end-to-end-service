# Product Spec — Meridian Transfers

## Overview

Meridian Transfers lets a business send money to vendors, contractors, and other
Meridian Pay customers. Transfers move money directly from the sender's Wallet.

## Transfer Types

There are two transfer types:

- **Instant Transfer**: arrives within seconds and is available 24 hours a day, seven
  days a week. Instant Transfers are only available between two Meridian Pay Wallets.
- **Standard Transfer**: sent to an external bank account and arrives within one to
  three business days.

## Limits

The default daily transfer limit is 50,000 dollars. Customers can request a higher limit
of up to 250,000 dollars per day after completing enhanced identity verification.

## Fees

Instant Transfers between Meridian Pay Wallets are free. Standard Transfers to an
external bank cost a flat fee of 1 dollar per transfer, regardless of amount.

## Scheduling

Transfers can be scheduled up to 90 days in advance. Recurring transfers can be set to
run daily, weekly, or monthly.

## Cancellation

A scheduled Standard Transfer can be canceled any time before it begins processing at
5:00 PM Central Time on the send date. Instant Transfers cannot be canceled once sent.
