# Meridian Pay — Data Retention Policy

## Transaction Records

Customer transaction records are retained for seven years to comply with financial
regulations. After seven years, records are permanently deleted.

## Support Tickets

Customer support tickets and their contents are retained for two years after the ticket
is closed, then deleted.

## Application Logs

Application and server logs are retained for 90 days. Logs are stored in encrypted form
and are never exported outside of company infrastructure.

## Closed Accounts

When a customer closes their account, personal profile data is deleted within 30 days.
Transaction records tied to the account are kept for the full seven-year retention period
as required by law.

## Backups

Database backups are taken every day and retained for 35 days. Backups are encrypted and
stored in a separate geographic region from the primary database.

## Deletion Requests

A customer may request deletion of their personal data at any time. Meridian Pay
completes verified deletion requests within 30 days, except for records the company is
legally required to keep.
