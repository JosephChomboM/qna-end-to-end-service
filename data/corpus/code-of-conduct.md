# Meridian Pay — Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Meridian Pay expects every employee to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with
respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflict of interest to their manager in writing.
This includes outside employment, financial interests in competitors, and personal
relationships that could affect business decisions.

## Outside Employment

Employees may hold a second job as long as it does not compete with Meridian Pay, does
not use company resources, and does not interfere with their duties. Outside employment
must be disclosed to the employee's manager before it begins.

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from vendors or partners only if the value is under 100
dollars. Any gift valued at 100 dollars or more must be declined or reported to the
compliance team.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company and customer information both during and
after their employment. This obligation continues indefinitely after an employee leaves.

## Reporting Violations

Suspected violations of this code may be reported anonymously through the company ethics
hotline. Meridian Pay prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith.
