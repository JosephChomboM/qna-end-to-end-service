# Product Spec — Meridian Wallet

## Overview

Meridian Wallet is a digital account that lets a small business hold, receive, and spend
money. Every Meridian Pay customer gets one Wallet when they sign up.

## Balance Limits

A standard Wallet can hold a maximum balance of 250,000 dollars. Businesses that need a
higher limit can apply for a Premium Wallet, which raises the limit to 1,000,000 dollars.

## Interest

Funds held in a Meridian Wallet earn 2.5 percent annual interest, paid monthly. Interest
is calculated on the average daily balance for the month.

## FDIC Insurance

Wallet balances are held at partner banks and are FDIC-insured up to 250,000 dollars per
customer.

## Sub-accounts

Each Wallet supports up to 20 sub-accounts, which businesses use to separate funds by
purpose, such as payroll, taxes, and operating expenses.

## Funding

A Wallet can be funded by bank transfer, debit card, or incoming Meridian Transfer. Bank
transfers post within one business day. Debit card deposits are instant but carry a 1
percent fee.
