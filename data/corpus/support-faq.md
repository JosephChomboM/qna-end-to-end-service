# Meridian Pay — Customer Support FAQ

## How do I contact support?

Support is available by in-app chat and email. Starter and Growth customers receive
support during business hours, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday.
Scale customers receive 24/7 priority support.

## How long does support take to respond?

The target first-response time is four hours for Starter and Growth customers and one
hour for Scale customers.

## How do I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in screen. A reset link is emailed to the
address on file and expires after 30 minutes.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Meridian Pay has apps for both iOS and Android. All account features are available
in the mobile app.

## How do I close my account?

You can close your account from Settings, but only after your Wallet balance reaches
zero. Closing an account is permanent and cannot be undone.

## What currencies are supported?

Meridian Pay currently supports only United States dollars (USD). Support for additional
currencies is not yet available.

## Where can I see my transaction history?

Full transaction history is available in the app and can be exported as a CSV or PDF file
for any date range within the past seven years.
