# Meridian Pay — Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual PTO Allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO
accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any
unused days beyond 5 are forfeited on December 31.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year.
Unused sick days do not carry over.

## Parental Leave

Meridian Pay offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents, regardless
of gender. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a
child.

## Holidays

Meridian Pay observes 11 paid company holidays each year. In addition, employees receive
two floating holidays that they may use on any day of their choosing.

## Requesting Time Off

PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for any absence longer than
three consecutive days. Shorter absences require 48 hours of notice.
