# Meridian Pay — Information Security Policy

## Passwords

All employee accounts must use a password of at least 14 characters. Passwords must be
stored in the company-approved password manager, 1Password. Sharing passwords over email
or chat is prohibited.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every internal system. Hardware security
keys are the required second factor for anyone with access to production systems.

## Device Encryption

Full-disk encryption must be enabled on every company laptop. The IT team verifies
encryption status monthly.

## Data Classification

Meridian Pay classifies data into three levels: Public, Internal, and Restricted.
Customer financial data is always classified as Restricted and may never be copied to a
personal device.

## Access Reviews

Access to production systems is reviewed every quarter. Any access that is no longer
needed is revoked within five business days of the review.

## Reporting Incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of
discovery through the dedicated #security-alerts channel.
