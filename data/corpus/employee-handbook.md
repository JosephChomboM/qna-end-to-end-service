# Meridian Pay — Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

Meridian Pay uses flexible working hours. Employees are expected to be available during
the core collaboration window of 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday.
Outside of that window, employees may set their own schedule.

## Standard Work Week

The standard work week is 40 hours. Overtime for hourly employees is paid at 1.5 times
the regular rate for any hours worked beyond 40 in a single week.

## Pay Schedule

Employees are paid twice per month, on the 15th and the last business day of each month.
Pay is delivered by direct deposit only.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews happen once per year, in November. Every employee also has a
lightweight check-in with their manager once per quarter.

## Probation Period

New employees are on a probation period for the first 90 days of employment. During
probation, either party may end employment with one week of notice.

## Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a candidate that is hired and stays for at least six months receive
a referral bonus of 3,000 dollars.
