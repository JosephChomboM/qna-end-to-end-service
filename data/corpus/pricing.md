# Meridian Pay — Pricing

Meridian Pay offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Scale.

## Starter

The Starter plan is free. It includes one Wallet, up to 5 sub-accounts, the Business
Card, and free Instant Transfers between Meridian Pay Wallets. Standard Transfers cost
1 dollar each.

## Growth

The Growth plan costs 49 dollars per month. It includes everything in Starter, plus up
to 20 sub-accounts, up to 10 team members, and 20 free Standard Transfers per month.
Additional Standard Transfers cost 1 dollar each.

## Scale

The Scale plan costs 199 dollars per month. It includes everything in Growth, plus
unlimited team members, unlimited free Standard Transfers, a Premium Wallet, and priority
support with a dedicated account manager.

## Annual Discount

Customers who pay annually instead of monthly receive two months free, which is
equivalent to a 16.7 percent discount.

## No Hidden Fees

There are no account opening fees, no minimum balance requirements, and no overdraft
fees on any plan.
